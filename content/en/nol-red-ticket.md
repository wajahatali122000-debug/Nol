The nol Red ticket is a paper ticket that costs AED 2. It is valid for 90 days or 10 trips, works for one type of transport at a time, and is paid per trip. It is best for a short stay or a few trips, not for daily commuting.

If you ride often, a [Silver card](/nol-silver-card) is cheaper per trip. Compare all options on the [card types page](/nol-card-types).

## Red ticket at a glance

| Feature | Red ticket |
|---|---|
| Price | AED 2 |
| Format | Paper ticket |
| Validity | 90 days or 10 trips |
| Transport | One type of transport at a time |
| Payment | Pay per trip |

## Red ticket fares

Dubai has 7 fare zones, and fares depend on how many zones you pass through.

| Trip | Red ticket regular | Red ticket Gold |
|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 4 | AED 8 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 6 | AED 12 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 8.50 | AED 17 |

The Gold column is for riders who use the Gold Class cabin. Read the [Dubai Metro Gold Class guide](/dubai-metro-gold-class) for who can use it.

## Red ticket vs Silver card

Compare the fare on each trip:

- Within 1 zone: AED 3 on Silver vs AED 4 on Red ticket.
- 2 adjacent zones: AED 5 on Silver vs AED 6 on Red ticket.
- More than 2 zones: AED 7.50 on Silver vs AED 8.50 on Red ticket.

On every zone band, the Silver fare is lower. The Red ticket's AED 2 price is the only reason to choose it for a few trips, since you do not need to buy a card. A Silver card costs AED 25 and includes AED 19 credit, so the credit is spent on your trips rather than lost.

## When is a Red ticket worth it?

A Red ticket is worth it if:

- You are visiting for a short time and take only a few trips.
- You do not want to buy a card.
- You only need one type of transport.

A Silver card is worth it if:

- You ride the Metro, Tram or bus regularly.
- You want to avoid paying the Red ticket price on every trip.
- You want a card you can register, which also protects your balance.

## Red ticket limits to know

- The ticket is valid for **10 trips or 90 days**. Plan your trips so you use it within this limit.
- It works for **one type of transport at a time**. Do not expect one Red ticket to cover a mix of Metro, Tram and bus in the same way a card does.
- It is paper, so keep it safe.

For how transfers are charged on other cards, see [metro and bus transfers](/nol-metro-bus-transfer).

## Gold Red ticket

The Gold Red ticket fares are AED 8, AED 12 and AED 17 for the same zone bands. You need the gold fare for the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram. The [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card) explains the Gold card option and its fares.

## Quick answers

- **Price:** AED 2 ticket. Each trip is paid at the Red ticket fare.
- **Validity:** 90 days or 10 trips.
- **Fares:** AED 4 / 6 / 8.50 regular, AED 8 / 12 / 17 Gold.
- **Best for:** a few trips, not daily travel.
