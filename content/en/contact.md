For questions, corrections or feedback about this site, email us at **hello@example.com**. We read every message and update pages when we find errors.

## Before you email

Please don't send your card number, PIN, bank details or any personal documents. We can't check your balance or process a refund, and we never ask for these details.

## For card and balance help

Nol Balance Guide is an independent site. For help with your card, contact the people who run the service:

- RTA call centre: **800 9090**
- rta.ae for the current rules, fares and card information
- nol.rta.ae for the nol portal

## What to include in your message

- The page you were reading
- What you think is wrong or missing
- A link to the source, if you have one

## Corrections

If a fare, card type or date on a page looks wrong, email us the page address and the figure you found on rta.ae. We check the change and update the page where needed.

## Scams and suspicious messages

If someone asks for your nol PIN or card details and says they are from this site, don't reply. We never ask for them. Report the message to RTA through rta.ae.

## Looking for something else?

Start with the [about page](/about) to see what the site covers, or read the [disclaimer](/disclaimer) before you act on any figure. The [terms of use](/terms) and [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) explain the rest.

## Response times

We aim to reply to emails as soon as we can. We may not be able to respond to every message.
