Every nol card has a limited validity. Silver, Gold and Personal (Blue) cards are valid for 5 years. A Red ticket is valid for 90 days or 10 trips. When a card expires, you can request a refund and move the balance. Then you can get a new card.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** most nol cards last 5 years. If yours has expired, request a refund on rta.ae and take the balance as cash or transfer it to another card. Renew or buy a new card on the nol portal.

</div>

## How long each card lasts

| Card | Validity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 5 years | Standard card |
| Gold | 5 years | Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram |
| Personal (Blue) | 5 years | Personalised with your name and photo |
| nol Travel card | 5 years | Aimed at tourists and residents |
| Red ticket | 90 days or 10 trips | Paper ticket, one type of transport at a time |

For full card details, see [nol card types](/nol-card-types).

## What to do when your card expires

For an expired card, use the refund process to get the balance back.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the refund option on the RTA website, at a Smart Kiosk or in the nol Pay app.</li>
<li>Select <strong>expired</strong> as the reason.</li>
<li>Enter the card details asked for. Have your registration PIN ready if you have one.</li>
<li>Choose cash collection, or transfer the balance to another registered or personal card.</li>
<li>Submit the request.</li>
</ol>

If you choose a transfer, the receiving card must be tapped at a parking meter, a solar-powered bus machine or a metro gate to receive the balance. For the full process, read the [nol card refund guide](/nol-card-refund).

## How to renew or get a new card

RTA runs renewals and new cards through the nol portal at [nol.rta.ae](https://nol.rta.ae/). The portal lets you:

- Apply for a card
- Personalise a card
- Top up a card
- Renew a travel pass

Check the portal for the steps that apply to your card type. RTA's cards page on rta.ae lists the card options too.

### Fees and prices

This guide doesn't list renewal or card prices, because they change over time. Check the current figures on rta.ae or the nol portal before you pay. If you're not sure what to buy, use the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

## Red tickets are different

A Red ticket is a paper ticket. It's valid for 90 days or 10 trips. It works for one type of transport at a time and is pay-per-trip.

Read more on the [Red ticket page](/nol-red-ticket). If you ride every day, a Silver, Gold or Personal (Blue) card is usually the better choice, because it lasts 5 years.

## Before your card expires

These steps can save you time later:

1. Check how much balance is left, and use it or plan to move it.
2. If the balance is large, consider a refund and transfer before the card expires.
3. Note the date you bought the card so you know when it ends.
4. Register the card if you haven't done so yet. See [register your nol card](/register-nol-card).

## Plan your next top-up

Once you have a new card, you can [recharge your nol card](/nol-card-recharge) at ticket machines, ticket offices, the nol Pay app, the RTA website or sales outlets. Remember that online top-ups need activation. Wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap the card at a metro gate, an RTA parking meter or a solar-powered bus machine.

## Common mistakes

- Letting a card expire with a large balance still on it. Plan the refund or transfer early.
- Assuming a Red ticket lasts as long as a 5-year card. It's valid for 90 days or 10 trips.
- Renewing without checking the current prices on rta.ae or nol.rta.ae.

## Renew or get a new card: which to choose

| Your situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Card is still valid | Keep using it and top up as normal |
| Card has expired | Request a refund for the balance, then get a new card |
| You want a personalised card | Look at the [personal (Blue) card](/nol-personal-blue-card). It is personalised and required for concession fares |
| You travel only now and then | Consider a [Red ticket](/nol-red-ticket), which is pay-per-trip |

## Questions about your card?

Call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**. If you lost the card before it expired, read the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card) first.
