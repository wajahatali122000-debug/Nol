The nol Gold card costs AED 25, includes AED 19 credit and is valid for 5 years. It lets you use the Gold Class cabin on the Metro and Tram. The trade-off is price: Gold fares are double the standard fares.

If you do not need the Gold Class cabin, the [Silver card](/nol-silver-card) is cheaper to ride. Compare all cards on the [card types page](/nol-card-types).

## Gold card at a glance

| Feature | Gold card |
|---|---|
| Price | AED 25 |
| Credit included | AED 19 |
| Validity | 5 years |
| Gold Class cabin | Yes, on Metro and Tram |
| Max balance (anonymous) | AED 1,000 |
| Max balance (registered) | AED 5,000 |

## Gold vs Silver fare table

Dubai has 7 fare zones, and fares depend on how many zones you pass through. Gold fares are double the standard fares.

| Trip | Silver fare | Gold fare |
|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 | AED 6 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 | AED 10 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 | AED 15 |

Use the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to see what a month of Gold trips costs. The full fare rules are in the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Example: Silver vs Gold for a daily commute

Take a commute of 2 trips a day, 22 working days a month, with each trip crossing 2 adjacent zones on the Metro.

- **Silver:** AED 5 a trip. 2 trips a day for 22 days is 44 trips, so AED 5 × 44 = **AED 220** a month.
- **Gold:** AED 10 a trip. The same 44 trips cost AED 10 × 44 = **AED 440** a month.

The Gold card costs AED 220 more per month for this commute. It only makes sense if you want the Gold Class cabin.

## When is the Gold card worth it?

The Gold card is worth it if:

- You want to ride in the Gold Class cabin on the Metro or Tram.
- You are happy to pay double fares for the extra comfort.

The Gold card is not worth it if:

- You only want the cheapest way to travel.
- You ride the bus most of the time. The Gold surcharge applies only on Metro and Tram.

## Gold surcharge rules

RTA's rules on the Gold surcharge are:

- The Gold surcharge applies on Metro and Tram only.
- If you transfer from the Tram to the Metro, you are not charged twice for the same zone.

For how transfers are charged in general, read [metro and bus transfers](/nol-metro-bus-transfer).

## Gold Class without a Gold card

You can also use the Gold Class cabin with a Red ticket at the gold fare. The Red ticket gold fares are AED 8, AED 12 and AED 17. See [the Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket) for how Red tickets work.

## How to get a Gold card

An anonymous Gold card can be registered online or at any metro ticket office, like a Silver card, and you can top up through the nol Pay app, ticket machines, offices and authorised outlets. Read the [card types page](/nol-card-types) for the full overview.

## Quick answers

- **Price:** AED 25, with AED 19 credit.
- **Validity:** 5 years.
- **Gold Class:** on Metro and Tram.
- **Gold fares:** AED 6 / 10 / 15, double the standard fares.
