Registering turns an anonymous nol card into a registered one. You get a PIN by SMS, then tap the card at a metro gate to activate it. In return, the card gets balance protection and a higher limit.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** register online at nol.rta.ae or at any metro ticket office, wait for the SMS PIN, then tap your card at a metro gate. Registered cards can hold up to AED 5,000.

</div>

## Anonymous vs registered

| Feature | Anonymous | Registered or Personal |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum balance | AED 1,000 | AED 5,000 |
| Traceable to an owner | No | Yes |
| Balance protected if lost | No | Yes |
| Refund and card management PIN | No | Yes, by SMS PIN |

Anonymous cards can't be recovered if lost. That's the main reason to register a card you plan to keep.

## How to register online

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a>.</li>
<li>Choose the option to register your card.</li>
<li>Enter the details the portal asks for, including your card's 10-digit tag ID. See <a href="/find-nol-card-number">where to find it</a>.</li>
<li>Submit the form and wait for approval.</li>
<li>Watch for the SMS with your PIN, and keep it safe.</li>
</ol>

## How to register at a ticket office

<ol class="steps">
<li>Take your anonymous Silver or Gold card to any metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Ask the staff to register it.</li>
<li>Give the details they ask for.</li>
<li>Wait for approval, then watch for the SMS with your PIN.</li>
</ol>

## Activate the registration by tapping

After approval, the card still needs one step before the Registered status applies.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to a metro station after you receive the SMS.</li>
<li>Tap your card at the metro gate to check in.</li>
<li>Tap it again at the gate to check out. This completes the activation.</li>
</ol>

<div class="tip">

**Tip:** register the card before you top it up to a high balance. Then the protection and the AED 5,000 limit both apply to the full amount.

</div>

## Why registering matters

- **Balance protection.** If you lose a registered card, the balance can be recovered. An anonymous card's balance cannot.
- **Higher limit.** Registered cards hold up to AED 5,000, against AED 1,000 for anonymous cards.
- **Refunds.** A refund can be paid as cash or moved to another registered or Personal card. The receiving card has to be tapped at a parking meter, a solar-powered bus machine or a metro gate to receive the balance.

Read the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card) for the reporting steps, and the [refund guide](/nol-card-refund) for the options.

## Keep your PIN private

The SMS PIN is used for refunds and for managing your cards. Don't share it. This guide never asks for your PIN, your card number or your bank details. Enter those only on rta.ae or nol.rta.ae.

## Personal card as an alternative

A Personal (Blue) card is personalised with your name and photo, and its balance can be recovered if it's lost. It costs AED 70, including AED 20 credit, and lasts 5 years. Compare it with your current card on the [Personal card page](/nol-personal-blue-card).

## Who should register

- Anyone keeping a card for months, not just a single trip.
- Anyone who wants to load more than AED 1,000, which an anonymous card can't hold.
- Anyone who would find it hard to replace a lost balance.

For short visits, the [tourist guide](/nol-card-for-tourists) compares the options.

## Why the tap matters

The tap at the gate is what changes the card to the Registered status. Until you tap, the protection and the higher limit may not apply yet, so do this step soon after you get the SMS.

## After you register

- Your balance is protected if the card is lost.
- The card can hold up to AED 5,000.
- Keep your PIN for refunds and card management.
- If the card is lost, report it quickly through the RTA website, Smart Kiosks or the nol Pay app. See the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card).

## Registration checklist

- Your anonymous Silver or Gold card.
- The 10-digit tag ID, written down. See [where to find it](/find-nol-card-number).
- A mobile phone that can receive SMS.
- Time to visit a metro station for the tap.
