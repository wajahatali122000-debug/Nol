Concession fares on nol are for specific groups, and you need a Personal (Blue) card to get them. The groups RTA lists are students, People of Determination, seniors aged 60 and over, and social-affairs beneficiaries. RTA does not publish the discount percentage on its nol card pages, so check the current figure with RTA before you apply.

## Concession groups

| Group | Card needed |
|---|---|
| Students | Personal (Blue) card |
| People of Determination | Personal (Blue) card |
| Seniors aged 60 and over | Personal (Blue) card |
| Social-affairs beneficiaries | Personal (Blue) card |

Eligibility is set by RTA, not by this guide. If you are not sure whether you qualify, ask RTA directly.

## Why you need a Personal card

RTA requires a Personal (Blue) card for concession fares. The card costs AED 70, includes AED 20 credit and is valid for 5 years. It is personalised with your name and photo. Read the [Personal (Blue) card guide](/nol-personal-blue-card) for the full details.

A Silver card costs AED 25 and does not give you concession fares. See the [Silver card guide](/nol-silver-card) for the standard fares it pays.

## How to apply

Use these steps to start your application. The exact documents can change, so check them before you go.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the nol portal at <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a> and find the option for a personalised card, or go to any metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Get a Personal (Blue) card. Ask the staff or check the portal for the documents you need.</li>
<li>Check with RTA that you qualify for the concession group you belong to.</li>
<li>Once the card is issued and your eligibility is confirmed, top it up and travel.</li>
</ol>

<div class="warn">

**Do not guess the discount.** RTA does not publish the percentage on its nol card pages, so this guide does not state one. Confirm the current figure with RTA.

</div>

## How to check with RTA

To confirm your eligibility or the current discount, contact RTA:

- Call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**.
- Check [rta.ae](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/choosenolcard) for the current nol card information.

When you call, have your Personal card details ready if you already have one. Never share your card number with third-party websites, including this one.

## Concession fares and standard fares

Concession fares sit on top of the standard fare structure. If you are not sure how the fare zones work, read the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares). It lists the standard Silver and Personal fares of AED 3, AED 5 and AED 7.50, which apply by zone.

You can use the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to see your standard monthly cost. Apply any concession discount yourself once RTA confirms your figure.

## News on nol Jaywan

Reports about the next-generation nol Jaywan card mention a large number of discounts, and a pilot planned from November 2026. RTA has not confirmed the full details, the launch date or the price. Read [the nol Jaywan card news](/nol-jaywan-card) for what is reported so far, and check RTA for confirmed information.

## Who to ask

- **Eligibility:** RTA, call 800 9090.
- **Card application:** nol.rta.ae or a metro ticket office.
- **Current discount figure:** RTA, rta.ae.

- **Card needed:** Personal (Blue).
- **Discount percentage:** not published by RTA on these pages, so check with RTA.
