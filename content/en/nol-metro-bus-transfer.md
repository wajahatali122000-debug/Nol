If you check out of the Metro and check in on a bus within 30 minutes, nol charges the trip as one journey. The fare is based on the total zones you pass through, not two separate fares.

This page explains the 30-minute rule, how the fare is worked out, and a few examples. For the full fare table, see the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## The 30-minute rule

RTA's transfer rule works like this:

- Check out of the Metro.
- Check in on a bus within 30 minutes.
- The journey is charged as one trip, based on the total zones.

If you go past 30 minutes, the bus tap is a new journey. Plan your connection so you tap in on the bus within the window.

## How the transfer is charged

Without a transfer, each leg is charged separately. With the transfer, the zones from both legs are added together and the combined fare applies.

For a Silver card, the fare bands are:

| Total zones in one transfer journey | Silver / Personal fare |
|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 |

The Gold card pays Gold fares, which are double the standard figures. The Gold surcharge rules are covered below, and the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card) has the Gold fare table.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Tap out at the Metro gate when you leave the station.</li>
<li>Walk to the bus stop and board your bus.</li>
<li>Tap your card on the bus reader within 30 minutes of tapping out.</li>
<li>The journey is charged as one trip based on the total zones.</li>
</ol>

## Worked examples

These examples use the standard Silver card fares. Your actual zones depend on your route, so check the zone map on rta.ae.

### Example 1: Metro then bus, 2 zones in total

You ride the Metro through 1 zone, then take a bus that passes through 1 more zone. The total is 2 zones.

- Without the transfer: AED 3 + AED 3 = AED 6.
- With the transfer: one journey over 2 adjacent zones = AED 5.

The transfer saves you AED 1 in this case.

### Example 2: Metro then bus, within 1 zone

Both legs stay inside 1 zone in total.

- With the transfer: one journey within 1 zone = AED 3.
- Without the transfer: you would pay AED 3 for each leg.

### Example 3: Transfer after 30 minutes

You tap out of the Metro, then tap in on the bus 40 minutes later.

- The bus tap is outside the 30-minute window.
- The bus trip is charged as a separate journey.

Keep the 30-minute limit in mind when you plan connections.

## Gold cards and transfers

The Gold surcharge applies on Metro and Tram only, not on buses. RTA does not give a separate Gold transfer example on the pages we used, so check rta.ae for how a Gold card transfer is charged. Read [Dubai Metro Gold Class](/dubai-metro-gold-class) for more on the Gold rules.

## Tram to Metro

RTA's rules also cover the Tram. When you transfer from the Tram to the Metro, you are not charged twice for the same zone. This is part of the Gold surcharge rule, so check [the fares guide](/nol-card-fares) for the full rule.

## Tips for smooth transfers

- Tap out at the Metro gate before you leave the station. The transfer needs that check-out.
- Keep your card in your hand so you can tap quickly at the bus reader.
- Check your balance before you travel. See [how to check your nol balance](/) if you are unsure.
- If you have a Red ticket, it works for one type of transport at a time, so check its limits on the [Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket).

## Quick answers

- **Window:** 30 minutes from Metro check-out to bus check-in.
- **Charge:** one journey, based on total zones.
- **Silver fares:** AED 3 / 5 / 7.50.
- **Gold fares:** AED 6 / 10 / 15.
