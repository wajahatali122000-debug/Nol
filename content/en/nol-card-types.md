Dubai has five nol cards and tickets. The **Silver card** is the standard choice for most daily riders. The **Red ticket** suits a few trips, and the **Personal (Blue) card** is the one you need for concession fares.

Use the table to compare them, then read the short notes below. If you want a quick recommendation, try our [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

## Comparison of all nol cards

| Card | Price | Credit included | Validity | Max balance | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Silver](/nol-silver-card) | AED 25 | AED 19 | 5 years | AED 1,000 anonymous / AED 5,000 registered | Everyday travel at standard fares |
| [Gold](/nol-gold-card) | AED 25 | AED 19 | 5 years | AED 1,000 anonymous / AED 5,000 registered | Riders who want the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram |
| [Personal (Blue)](/nol-personal-blue-card) | AED 70 | AED 20 | 5 years | AED 5,000 | Concession fares, a personalised card, balance protection |
| [Red ticket](/nol-red-ticket) | AED 2 | None (pay per trip) | 90 days or 10 trips | Not applicable | A few trips, one type of transport |
| [nol Travel](/nol-travel-card) | AED 100 | AED 19 | 5 years | Check noltravel.ae | Tourists and residents, per RTA's site |

Max balance figures come from RTA's published rules. The Travel card limit is not on our facts list, so check it on noltravel.ae.

## Silver card

The Silver card is RTA's standard card. It costs AED 25, includes AED 19 credit and lasts 5 years. It pays standard fares, which start at AED 3 for one zone.

Read the full [Silver card guide](/nol-silver-card) for fares, registration and top-up steps.

## Gold card

The Gold card costs the same as Silver: AED 25 including AED 19 credit, valid for 5 years. The difference is the fares. Gold fares are double the standard fares, and the card lets you use the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram.

If you do not need the cabin, the Silver card is the better choice. See the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card) for the full Gold vs Silver fare table.

## Personal (Blue) card

The Personal card costs AED 70 including AED 20 credit and is valid for 5 years. It is personalised with your name and photo, and RTA says the balance can be recovered if the card is lost. It is also required for concession fares.

Read the [Personal (Blue) card guide](/nol-personal-blue-card) for how to apply. For who qualifies for concessions, see [nol card discounts](/nol-card-discounts).

## Red ticket

The Red ticket is a paper ticket that costs AED 2. It is valid for 90 days or 10 trips, works for one type of transport at a time, and is paid per trip. It can make sense for a short visit or a few trips.

Read the [Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket) to see when it is worth it compared with a Silver card.

## nol Travel card

The nol Travel card costs AED 100 including AED 19 credit and is valid for 5 years. RTA positions it for tourists and residents and runs a separate site for it, noltravel.ae. We do not list its extra benefits here, so check that site before you buy.

Read the [nol Travel card page](/nol-travel-card) for the details we can confirm.

## Which card should you pick?

- **Daily commute, standard fares:** Silver.
- **You want the Gold Class cabin:** Gold.
- **You need concession fares or a personalised card:** Personal (Blue).
- **A few trips and no card:** Red ticket.
- **You are visiting and want RTA's travel card:** nol Travel.

## Registering your card

Anonymous Silver and Gold cards can be registered online on the RTA portal or at any metro ticket office. Registering protects your balance if the card is lost, and it raises the maximum balance to AED 5,000. Read [how to register a nol card](/register-nol-card) for the steps.

## Fares on each card

Standard fares on the Silver and Personal cards are AED 3, AED 5 and AED 7.50 for 1 zone, 2 adjacent zones and more than 2 zones. Gold fares are AED 6, AED 10 and AED 15. The full table for all cards is in the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Quick answer for each card

- Silver and Gold: AED 25, AED 19 credit, 5 years.
- Personal (Blue): AED 70, AED 20 credit, 5 years, needed for concessions.
- Red ticket: AED 2, 90 days or 10 trips, pay per trip.
- nol Travel: AED 100, AED 19 credit, 5 years.
