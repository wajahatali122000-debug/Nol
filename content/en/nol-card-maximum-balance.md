Every nol card has a balance ceiling. An anonymous card can hold up to **AED 1,000**. A registered or Personal card can hold up to **AED 5,000**. To go above AED 1,000, register your card or get a Personal card.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** anonymous cards cap at AED 1,000. Registered and Personal cards cap at AED 5,000.

</div>

## Balance limits by card

| Card | Maximum balance | If the card is lost |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous Silver or Gold | AED 1,000 | Balance can't be recovered, and the card can't be traced |
| Registered Silver or Gold | AED 5,000 | Balance is protected, and the card can be traced |
| Personal (Blue) | AED 5,000 | Balance can be recovered |

## Option 1: register your current card

If you already have an anonymous Silver or Gold card, register it. The limit rises to AED 5,000 once the card is registered and you've tapped it at a metro gate to activate the status.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Register online at <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a>, or at any metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Wait for the SMS with your PIN.</li>
<li>Tap the card at a metro gate to check in and check out.</li>
</ol>

Full steps are in the [register your nol card guide](/register-nol-card).

## Option 2: get a Personal card

A Personal (Blue) card is a new card with a higher limit built in. It's personalised with your name and photo.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a> and choose the Personal card application.</li>
<li>Add your name and photo, as the portal asks.</li>
<li>Complete the payment and follow the steps to receive the card.</li>
</ol>

The card costs AED 70, including AED 20 credit, and lasts 5 years. Read the [Personal (Blue) card guide](/nol-personal-blue-card) for the full details.

## Compare the two options

| | Register your current card | Get a Personal card |
|---|---|---|
| Starting point | Anonymous card you already have | New card |
| Price | RTA's nol pages don't list a registration fee. Check rta.ae. | AED 70, including AED 20 credit |
| Maximum balance | AED 5,000 | AED 5,000 |
| Balance recoverable if lost | Yes, once registered | Yes |

## Plan top-ups around the limit

- **Check your balance first.** Work out what's on the card before you add more.
- **Top up in smaller amounts if you travel often.** This keeps the balance well below the ceiling.
- **Know your fare.** The highest standard Silver fare is AED 7.50. At that rate, AED 1,000 covers more than 130 trips.

Use the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to plan the amount, or read the [nol card recharge guide](/nol-card-recharge) for every top-up channel.

## What the limit means in practice

- At the highest standard Silver fare of AED 7.50, a full AED 1,000 covers more than 130 trips.
- Anonymous card balances can't be recovered if the card is lost. Keep the balance on an anonymous card low.
- A registered or Personal card can hold more, but only if it's protected. Register it before you load a large amount.

## Tips for keeping a large balance safe

- Register or personalise the card before you load a large amount.
- Keep the card separate from your PIN and tag ID. Store the tag ID somewhere safe.
- Check the balance after each top-up, so you know the card holds what you expect.
- Report a lost card straight away. A registered or Personal card can be traced to its owner.

## Lost card and the limit

If a registered or Personal card is lost, you can apply for a refund. The balance can be cashed out or moved to another registered or Personal card. The receiving card must be tapped at a parking meter, a solar-powered bus machine or a metro gate. Read the [refund guide](/nol-card-refund) for the steps.

## Which option fits you

- **You already have an anonymous card:** register it, then raise the limit.
- **You want a new card with protection built in:** get a Personal card.
- **You only need a small balance:** the AED 1,000 limit on an anonymous card may be enough.

## If your top-up doesn't show

A top-up that has been paid but doesn't appear may be waiting for activation. Online top-ups need 45 minutes to 4 hours, then a tap at a metro gate, RTA parking meter or solar-powered bus machine. If the balance still hasn't changed after that, follow the [balance not updated guide](/nol-balance-not-updated) or call RTA on 800 9090.
