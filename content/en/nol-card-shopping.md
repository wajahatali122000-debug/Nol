Yes, you can pay with a nol card at participating outlets. RTA sets a cap of AED 200 per transaction. The card works best for small everyday purchases, such as breakfast, groceries, snacks and fuel. Check with the cashier before you pay, because acceptance depends on the outlet.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** tap or present your nol card at a participating outlet, keep the purchase within AED 200 per transaction, and make sure the balance covers it.

</div>

## What you can buy with nol

RTA lists these types of outlets as examples of where nol can be used:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | Quick food and drinks in the morning |
| Groceries | Everyday food and household items |
| Snacks | Small items for the road |
| Fuel | Petrol and fuel top-ups |

Outlets vary. Some shops take nol and others don't, so ask before you add items to your basket.

## The AED 200 cap

RTA states a cap of **AED 200 per transaction** for nol payments at outlets. A single purchase above AED 200 can't be paid in full with nol in one transaction. Plan larger purchases with another payment method.

Keep these points in mind:

- The cap is per transaction. Check rta.ae for any other current limits.
- If your total is above the cap, pay the difference another way.
- Check the receipt to confirm the amount that was taken from the card.

## How to pay with nol at a shop

<ol class="steps">
<li>Check that the outlet accepts nol. Look for signage or ask the cashier.</li>
<li>Check that your card has enough balance for the purchase.</li>
<li>Make sure the total is within AED 200.</li>
<li>Tap your card on the reader and follow the screen prompts.</li>
<li>Keep the receipt so you can check the amount taken from your card.</li>
</ol>

## Top up before you shop

Keep your card topped up so you don't get stuck at the till. You can [recharge your nol card](/nol-card-recharge) through the nol Pay app, the RTA website, the RTA Dubai and S'hail apps, ticket machines, ticket offices and authorised sales outlets.

Online top-ups need activation before the balance is available. Wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap the card at a metro gate, an RTA parking meter or a solar-powered bus machine. If your balance hasn't updated, read [nol balance not updated](/nol-balance-not-updated).

## Check your balance first

The easiest way to avoid a failed payment is to check the balance before you shop. You can check it:

- On the nol Pay app, which also supports top-ups. See the [nol Pay app guide](/nol-pay-app).
- On the RTA website, using the 10-digit tag ID on the back of your card.
- At a ticket vending machine, by placing the card on the reader.

## Shopping and travel on one card

The same nol card can be used for Metro, Tram, bus, water bus, RTA paid parking and participating shops. Keeping one card helps you track your spending. For transport costs, see the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares). For parking, read [how to pay for RTA parking with nol](/nol-card-parking).

## Keep the card safe

Your card is linked to your balance, so protect it the same way you would protect a bank card:

- Register the card so you can recover the balance if it's lost. See [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card).
- Don't share your card details or PIN on sites that aren't rta.ae or nol.rta.ae.
- If you lose the card, read the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card).

## If a payment doesn't go through

- Check that the balance covers the purchase.
- Check that the total is within AED 200.
- Ask the cashier whether the outlet takes nol for that type of item.
- Pay the rest of the bill another way if the total is over the cap.

## Top-up outlets and shopping outlets are different

Top-up outlets sell and reload nol cards. Shopping outlets accept nol as payment for goods. A place can do one without the other, so check the purpose before you go.

## Questions about shopping with nol?

For the current shopping rules, check rta.ae. For card or balance problems, call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**.
