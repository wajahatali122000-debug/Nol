Visiting Dubai for a few days? Pick your card by how many trips you'll take. A red ticket suits a few short trips. A Silver card suits several trips. The nol Travel card is aimed at visitors and costs more up front.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** a few trips, buy a red ticket. Several trips, buy a Silver card. Then check the fare with the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

</div>

## Compare the three cards

| Card | Cost | Valid | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red ticket | AED 2 | 90 days or 10 trips | A few short trips, one type of transport at a time |
| Silver | AED 25 (AED 19 credit) | 5 years | Several trips across the stay |
| nol Travel card | AED 100 (AED 19 credit) | 5 years | Visitors who want the travel card |

## Fares: red ticket vs Silver

Red ticket fares are higher per trip than Silver fares. The gap matters if you ride more than a couple of times.

| Trip | Silver | Red ticket |
|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 | AED 4 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 | AED 6 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 | AED 8.50 |

Work out your total with the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator), or read the [nol fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Quick start steps

<ol class="steps">
<li><strong>Count your trips.</strong> Note how many journeys you expect, and whether they cross one zone or several.</li>
<li><strong>Pick your card.</strong> Use the <a href="/tools/which-nol-card">which nol card tool</a> if you're unsure.</li>
<li><strong>Buy it.</strong> Get it from a metro ticket office, a ticket vending machine or an authorised outlet. See <a href="/where-to-buy-nol-card">where to buy a nol card</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Add credit.</strong> Top up at a machine or ticket office, which doesn't need the online activation wait.</li>
<li><strong>Tap in.</strong> Tap at the metro gate, or on the bus, tram or water bus.</li>
<li><strong>Check the balance.</strong> Use a ticket machine if you want to see what's left.</li>
</ol>

## Tips for visitors

- **Top up at a machine.** Credit added at a ticket vending machine or ticket office is on the card straight away.
- **Transfers can save money.** If you check out of the metro and check in on a bus within 30 minutes, the journey is charged as one trip, by total zones.
- **Gold Class costs more.** Gold fares are double standard fares, and the Gold surcharge applies only on Metro and Tram.
- **Keep the balance in range.** An anonymous card holds up to AED 1,000. Read the [maximum balance guide](/nol-card-maximum-balance) before a large top-up.

## Example costs

- **One trip within 1 zone on Silver:** AED 3. On a red ticket: AED 4.
- **Two trips within 1 zone on Silver:** AED 6. On a red ticket: AED 8.
- **One trip across more than 2 zones on Silver:** AED 7.50. On a red ticket: AED 8.50.

The AED 25 Silver price includes AED 19 credit, so the first trips come out of that credit.

## If you're staying longer

If you're staying several weeks, a Silver card keeps your regular trips at standard fares. Top up in amounts you expect to use, and keep the balance under the AED 1,000 anonymous limit.

## When to register your card

A tourist card doesn't need registration to ride. If you'll keep the card and add a lot of credit, though, registration protects the balance if the card is lost, and lifts the limit to AED 5,000. Steps are in the [register your nol card guide](/register-nol-card).

## The nol Travel card

The nol Travel card costs AED 100, including AED 19 credit, and lasts 5 years. RTA aims it at tourists and residents. Check the full details on the [nol Travel card page](/nol-travel-card) before you buy, because this guide doesn't list its benefits.

## Getting around with a card

- **Metro:** tap at the gate when you enter and when you leave.
- **Bus, tram and water bus:** tap your card when you board.
- **Transfers:** if you change from the metro to a bus, check the fare rules on the [fares page](/nol-card-fares) so you know how the journey is charged.
- **Gold Class:** the Gold Class cabin is only on Metro and Tram, so you need a Gold card for it.

## Where to top up while you're there

You can [recharge your nol card](/nol-card-recharge) at ticket machines, metro ticket offices, authorised outlets and through the nol Pay app. Online top-ups need 45 minutes to 4 hours of activation before you tap the card.
