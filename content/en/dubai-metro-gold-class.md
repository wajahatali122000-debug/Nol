Gold Class is the premium cabin on Dubai's Metro and Tram. You can ride it with a nol Gold card, or with a Red ticket at the gold fare. Gold fares are double the standard fares, and the Gold surcharge applies on Metro and Tram only.

This guide covers who can use the cabin, what it costs and how the surcharge rules work. For the wider fare picture, read the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Who can use Gold Class

You can use the Gold Class cabin if you have either:

- **A nol Gold card.** The card costs AED 25, includes AED 19 credit and is valid for 5 years. See the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card).
- **A Red ticket at the gold fare.** The Red ticket costs AED 2 and is valid for 10 trips or 90 days. See the [Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket).

A Silver or Personal card does not give you Gold Class access. Those cards pay the standard fares.

## Gold Class fares

| Trip | Gold card | Red ticket Gold |
|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 6 | AED 8 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 10 | AED 12 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 15 | AED 17 |

For comparison, Silver and Personal cards pay AED 3, AED 5 and AED 7.50 for the same zone bands. The [card types page](/nol-card-types) compares all the cards side by side.

## Which option is cheaper?

- **Gold card:** the better choice if you ride Gold Class often. The card's fares are lower than the Red ticket gold fares on every zone band.
- **Red ticket gold:** a simple option for a short visit or a few trips, with no card needed.

For a worked monthly example, see the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card).

## How the Gold surcharge works

RTA's rules on the Gold surcharge are:

- The Gold surcharge applies on Metro and Tram only.
- It does not apply on buses or water buses.
- When you transfer from the Tram to the Metro, you are not charged twice for the same zone.

The surcharge is why the Gold fares are double the standard fares. The rules above stop you paying twice for the same zone on a Tram-to-Metro change.

## Tram to Metro transfers

Here is how the rule works in practice:

<ol class="steps">
<li>Ride the Tram and tap out at your Tram stop.</li>
<li>Change to the Metro and tap in at the Metro gate.</li>
<li>RTA's rule means the same zone is not charged twice.</li>
</ol>

If you are changing from the Metro to a bus, the 30-minute rule applies instead. Read the [Metro to bus transfer guide](/nol-metro-bus-transfer).

## What you need before you travel

- A Gold card with enough balance for the fare, or a Red ticket with trips left.
- A check of the Metro and Tram timings on rta.ae. We do not list timings here, because they can change.
- Your card ready for the gate and reader.

Top up your Gold card before the trip, and remember that online top-ups need activation. Read [how to recharge a nol card](/nol-card-recharge) for the options.

## Gold Class and a Silver card

If you already have a Silver card and only ride Gold Class now and then, buy a Red ticket and pay the gold fare for those trips. If you ride Gold Class most days, the Gold card is the better option. Compare the two on the [card types page](/nol-card-types).

## Quick answers

- **Who can use Gold Class:** Gold card holders, or Red ticket riders paying the gold fare.
- **Gold card fares:** AED 6 / 10 / 15.
- **Red ticket gold fares:** AED 8 / 12 / 17.
- **Surcharge:** Metro and Tram only, with no double charge for the same zone on a Tram-to-Metro transfer.
