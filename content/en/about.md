Nol Balance Guide is an independent website that explains the Dubai nol card in plain English. We cover how to check your balance, top up, pay for parking, get a refund and choose a card.

## Who runs this site

Nol Balance Guide is run by an independent publisher based in the UAE. We are not part of RTA, nol or Al Etihad Payments. We do not sell nol cards, and we do not process top-ups or refunds.

## What this site covers

- How to check your nol card balance
- How to top up and register your card
- Card types, fares and where to pay
- Lost card, refund and expiry steps
- News about future nol cards, clearly marked as reported

## How we keep information accurate

We check fares, card types and process details against information on rta.ae. Fees, timelines and details that RTA doesn't publish are left out or marked as unconfirmed. Rules can change, so always check rta.ae before you act on anything here.

## Our approach

- Plain language and short sentences
- No hype and no guesswork
- Clear labels when something is reported, not confirmed

## Why we built it

Nol rules, fares and card types are spread across several pages and apps. We group the key facts in one place, with links to the pages where you can check the details yourself.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell nol cards or process top-ups and refunds.
- We don't ask for card numbers, PINs or bank details.
- We don't claim to be RTA, nol or Al Etihad Payments.

## Get in touch

Questions or corrections? Visit the [contact page](/contact). For card and balance problems, contact the RTA call centre on 800 9090.
