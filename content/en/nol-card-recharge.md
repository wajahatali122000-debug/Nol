You can add credit to your nol card in several places. Ticket machines and ticket offices put the money on the card straight away. Online top-ups from the RTA website and apps need one activation tap before the new amount shows.

<!-- cta -->

## Top-up channels at a glance

| Channel | Activation wait? | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket vending machine (metro stations) | No | Topping up on the way |
| Metro ticket office | No | Topping up with staff help |
| Authorised sales outlets (2,000+) | Check the receipt and rta.ae | Topping up near home or work |
| nol Pay app | Not stated on RTA's nol pages | Managing your card on your phone |
| RTA website, RTA Dubai app, S'hail app | Yes: 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap | Topping up from home |

## Top up at a ticket vending machine

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to a ticket vending machine at a metro station.</li>
<li>Place your card on the reader and check the current balance.</li>
<li>Choose the top-up option, select the amount and pay as the screen asks.</li>
<li>Check the balance again before you tap in.</li>
</ol>

## Top up at a ticket office

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to a metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Hand over your card and tell the staff the amount you want to add.</li>
<li>Pay, then check the balance on the card before you leave.</li>
</ol>

## Top up online (RTA website or apps)

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the nol top-up option on the RTA website, the RTA Dubai app or the S'hail app.</li>
<li>Enter your 10-digit tag ID. See <a href="/find-nol-card-number">where to find it</a>.</li>
<li>Choose the amount and pay.</li>
<li>Wait <strong>45 minutes to 4 hours</strong>.</li>
<li>Tap your card at a metro gate, an RTA parking meter or a solar-powered bus machine.</li>
</ol>

<div class="note">

**Activation:** online top-ups from the RTA website, RTA Dubai app and S'hail app are not on the card until you tap it after the wait. Ticket machine and office top-ups are instant.

</div>

## Top up in the nol Pay app

nol Pay is RTA's dedicated nol app. It supports top-ups and a virtual nol card.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Install nol Pay from the <a href="https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/smart-apps/app-details/nol+pay/nol-pay" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol Pay page on rta.ae</a>.</li>
<li>Add your card by following the app prompts.</li>
<li>Choose the top-up option, enter the amount and pay.</li>
</ol>

For set-up details, read the [nol Pay app guide](/nol-pay-app).

## Top up at an authorised sales outlet

RTA lists more than 2,000 authorised sales outlets around metro stations and beyond. Ask the cashier to top up your nol card, and check the balance before you leave the shop. If the top-up doesn't show, follow the steps in the [balance not updated guide](/nol-balance-not-updated).

## Maximum balance

The balance ceiling depends on the card you hold. Plan your top-ups around it.

| Card | Maximum balance |
|---|---|
| Anonymous Silver or Gold | AED 1,000 |
| Registered Silver or Gold | AED 5,000 |
| Personal (Blue) | AED 5,000 |

To raise your limit, [register your card](/register-nol-card) or read the [maximum balance guide](/nol-card-maximum-balance).

## Which channel should you use?

- **Need the money on the card now:** use a ticket vending machine or a metro ticket office.
- **Topping up from home:** use the RTA website, or the RTA Dubai or S'hail app. Then wait 45 minutes to 4 hours and tap the card.
- **Managing the card on your phone:** use the nol Pay app.
- **Topping up near home or work:** use an authorised sales outlet.

## Recharge tips

- Check the balance before you top up, so you know how much room you have under your card's limit.
- Keep the receipt until the new amount shows.
- After an online top-up, tap the card before you rely on it for a trip.
- If the amount is still missing after the wait, follow the [nol balance not updated guide](/nol-balance-not-updated).

## Keep your card topped up

Top up before a busy week of travel, not on the morning you need the card. If you top up online, allow the 45 minutes to 4 hours and the tap before you rely on the new amount. A ticket machine is the fastest fallback if you're running low.

## Plan how far your top-up goes

Standard Silver fares depend on the number of zones you cross: AED 3, AED 5 or AED 7.50. Gold Class fares are double. Work out your monthly spend with the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator), or read the full [nol fares guide](/nol-card-fares).
