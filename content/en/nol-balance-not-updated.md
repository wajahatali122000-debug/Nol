Topped up, but the balance hasn't changed? Most of the time the cause is the activation step, not a failed payment. Online top-ups need 45 minutes to 4 hours, then one tap on a reader, before the new amount shows.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** if you paid online, wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap your card at a metro gate, an RTA parking meter or a solar-powered bus machine.

</div>

<!-- cta -->

## Which top-ups need activation

| Channel | Activation needed? |
|---|---|
| RTA website | Yes: wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap |
| RTA Dubai app | Yes: wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap |
| S'hail app | Yes: wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap |
| Ticket vending machine | No: the balance updates straight away |
| Ticket office | No: the balance updates straight away |

RTA's nol pages don't give a separate activation time for the nol Pay app. If a nol Pay top-up is missing, check the app and [the nol Pay guide](/nol-pay-app), or call RTA.

## Step by step: fix a missing online top-up

<ol class="steps">
<li>Note the time you paid and the channel you used.</li>
<li>Wait 45 minutes to 4 hours from the time of payment.</li>
<li>Tap your card at a <strong>metro gate</strong>, an <strong>RTA parking meter</strong> or a <strong>solar-powered bus machine</strong>.</li>
<li>Check the balance again on the nol site or at a ticket machine.</li>
<li>Still missing? Call RTA on <strong>800 9090</strong>.</li>
</ol>

## Why the balance can look unchanged after you pay

For online top-ups, the money is paid first and written to the card later, at the tap. Until that tap happens, the card still holds the old amount, so the balance looks unchanged even though the payment went through. Waiting and tapping is the step most people miss.

Keep a short record of each top-up:

- The date and time you paid.
- The channel you used (RTA website, RTA Dubai app or S'hail app).
- The amount, and the receipt or confirmation.
- The reader where you tapped, and the time.

## Double-check your tag ID first

Before you panic, make sure the balance check used the right card. Compare each digit of the 10-digit tag ID with the back of your card. A single wrong digit can show no result or a different card. The full guide is [where to find your nol card number](/find-nol-card-number).

## Check a ticket machine or office top-up

A top-up at a ticket machine or ticket office should show straight away. If it doesn't:

<ol class="steps">
<li>Keep the receipt from the machine or office.</li>
<li>Place the card on the reader again and check the balance.</li>
<li>If the amount still isn't there, take the card and receipt to a metro ticket office, or call RTA on 800 9090.</li>
</ol>

## Where a tap writes the top-up to your card

Any one of these readers works for the activation tap:

- A **metro gate**.
- An **RTA parking meter**.
- A **solar-powered bus machine**.

Tap once, then check the balance. You don't need to tap at every reader.

## When to call RTA

Call RTA on **800 9090** if all of these are true:

- At least 4 hours have passed since you paid.
- You tapped the card at a metro gate, parking meter or solar-powered bus machine.
- The balance still hasn't changed.

Have ready the time of payment, the channel you used, the 10-digit tag ID and proof of payment.

<div class="tip">

**Tip:** if you're unsure, top up at a ticket machine. Those top-ups go on the card straight away, so you can see the change at once.

</div>

## Other reasons the balance looks wrong

- **Balance limit reached.** An anonymous card holds up to AED 1,000. A top-up that goes over that ceiling may not add up as you expect. Read the [maximum balance guide](/nol-card-maximum-balance).
- **Card not registered.** Registering protects the balance and raises the limit to AED 5,000. See [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card).
- **Fare already taken.** A trip or a transfer takes money off the balance. Check your recent trips before you assume the top-up is missing.

## Plan your next top-up

Once the balance shows, work out how far it goes. Standard Silver fares are AED 3, AED 5 or AED 7.50 per trip, depending on zones. Use the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to plan, or read the [nol card recharge guide](/nol-card-recharge) before your next top-up.
