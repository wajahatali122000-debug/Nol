You can pay for RTA paid parking by tapping your nol card at the parking meter. Silver, Gold and Blue cards all work. According to [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/dubai-phase-out-cash-payments-parking-metres-june-1-2026), cash payments at parking meters were phased out from 1 June 2026, so nol is now one of the main ways to pay.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** walk up to any RTA parking meter, tap your Silver, Gold or Blue nol card on the reader, and follow the prompts on the screen.

</div>

## Which cards work for parking

| Card | Pay parking by tapping at the meter? |
|---|---|
| Silver | Yes |
| Gold | Yes |
| Blue (Personal) | Yes |
| Red ticket | Not confirmed in this guide. Check rta.ae. |

For a full comparison of card types, see [nol card types](/nol-card-types). If you're unsure which card to carry, use the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

## How to pay for parking with nol

<ol class="steps">
<li>Find an RTA parking meter in the zone where you're parking.</li>
<li>Tap your nol card on the reader and keep it there until the meter responds.</li>
<li>Follow the prompts on the screen to complete the payment.</li>
</ol>

If the card doesn't respond, check the balance first. The balance may be too low, or a recent top-up may still need activation. See the section on top-ups below.

## Cash phase-out from 1 June 2026

Khaleej Times reported that cash payments at Dubai parking meters were phased out from 1 June 2026. nol remains a payment option. Plan to pay with your nol card. If you usually carry cash for parking, load your card before you park.

## Top up before you park

Load your card before you head out, so you don't have to look for a top-up point while you're parked. You can [recharge your nol card](/nol-card-recharge) through these channels:

- nol Pay app
- Ticket offices and ticket vending machines
- The RTA website and the RTA Dubai and S'hail apps
- Authorised sales outlets around metro stations and beyond

## Top-ups made online need activation

Top-ups made on the RTA website, or in the RTA Dubai and S'hail apps, need activation before the balance is available on the card:

1. Wait **45 minutes to 4 hours** after paying.
2. Tap the card at an **RTA parking meter**, a **metro gate** or a **solar-powered bus machine**.
3. The new amount is written to the card.

You can do this step at a parking meter, so you don't need to travel first. If the balance still hasn't updated, see [nol balance not updated](/nol-balance-not-updated).

## Parking and transport on one card

The same nol card works for Metro, Tram, bus, water bus and RTA paid parking. One card covers your travel and your parking, so you only need to keep one balance topped up. For transport fares, see the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Tips for regular parkers

- Keep a small buffer on the card so a short parking session doesn't fail.
- Register your card, so the balance is protected if you lose it. Read [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card).
- Check your balance on the RTA website or in the nol Pay app if you're unsure.

## If the card doesn't work at the meter

Work through these checks before you give up on the meter:

- **Check the balance.** Use the RTA website, the nol Pay app or a ticket machine.
- **Activate a recent online top-up.** If you topped up on the RTA website or in the RTA Dubai or S'hail apps in the last 4 hours, tap the card at the meter to activate it. Wait at least 45 minutes first.
- **Check the card type.** This guide confirms parking for Silver, Gold and Blue cards only.
- **Call for help.** If the problem continues, call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**.
