On a Silver or Personal card, a trip costs AED 3 within 1 zone, AED 5 for 2 adjacent zones and AED 7.50 for more than 2 zones. Dubai has 7 fare zones, and your fare depends on how many zones you pass through.

Gold and Red ticket fares are higher. The table below shows every card, and the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) can estimate a month of trips.

## Full fare table for every card

| Trip | Silver / Personal | Gold | Red ticket regular | Red ticket Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 | AED 6 | AED 4 | AED 8 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 | AED 10 | AED 6 | AED 12 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 | AED 15 | AED 8.50 | AED 17 |

Silver and Personal cards pay the same fares. The Gold card and Gold Red ticket are for the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram. See the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card) and the [Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket) for who can use each.

## How the 7 fare zones work

Dubai's network is split into 7 fare zones. When you tap in and out, the system counts the zones you pass through and charges the matching fare:

- **Within 1 zone:** the lowest fare band.
- **2 adjacent zones:** the middle band.
- **More than 2 zones:** the highest standard band.

The number of zones passed is what sets the fare.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Tap in at the start station or stop.</li>
<li>Tap out at the destination. The system works out the zones passed.</li>
<li>The matching fare for your card is taken from the balance.</li>
</ol>

## Transfer rule

RTA's rule for transfers is simple:

- Check out of the Metro.
- Check in on a bus within 30 minutes.
- The trip is charged as one journey, based on the total zones.

Read [nol metro and bus transfers](/nol-metro-bus-transfer) for the details and examples.

## Gold surcharge rule

Gold fares apply on Metro and Tram only, and RTA's rules say:

- The Gold surcharge applies on Metro and Tram only, not on buses.
- If you transfer from the Tram to the Metro, you are not charged twice for the same zone.

For the Gold Class cabin itself, read [Dubai Metro Gold Class](/dubai-metro-gold-class).

## Worked example: a monthly commute

Here is a simple monthly estimate. Say you take 2 trips a day, 22 days a month, and each trip crosses 2 adjacent zones on a Silver card.

- Trips per month: 2 trips a day × 22 days = **44 trips**.
- Cost per trip: AED 5 for 2 adjacent zones.
- Monthly total: 44 × AED 5 = **AED 220**.

If the same trips were on a Gold card, each one would cost AED 10, so 44 × AED 10 = AED 440 a month. The [Gold card page](/nol-gold-card) compares the two in more detail.

## Worked example: a short visit

Say you take 4 trips on a Red ticket, each within 1 zone.

- Trip fares: 4 × AED 4 = **AED 16**.
- Plus the Red ticket price of AED 2.

For a short visit this may be simpler than buying a card. Read the [Red ticket guide](/nol-red-ticket) for the validity limits.

## Other fare questions

- **Can I check the current fares?** Yes. RTA publishes the fare table on its nol fares page, and the figures above are RTA's standard fares.
- **Is there a daily fare cap?** We could not confirm one from RTA's published pages, so do not assume one. Check rta.ae.
- **Which card should I choose?** For daily travel, the Silver card is usually the cheapest. Read the [card types comparison](/nol-card-types).

## Quick answers

- **Silver / Personal:** AED 3 / 5 / 7.50.
- **Gold:** AED 6 / 10 / 15.
- **Red ticket regular:** AED 4 / 6 / 8.50.
- **Red ticket Gold:** AED 8 / 12 / 17.
- **Transfers:** metro to bus within 30 minutes, charged as one journey.
