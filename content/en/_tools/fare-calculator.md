## How to use the result

The **per month** figure is roughly what to top up each month. Fares are charged per one-way trip, so a return commute counts as 2 trips a day.

Not sure how many zones you cross? A short hop within one area (for example within Deira) is usually 1 zone. Crossing from one side of the city to the other is usually more than 2. The ticket machine and your card receipt show the zones charged.
