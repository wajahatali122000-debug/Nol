  - q: "Which nol card is best for tourists?"

  - q: "What's the difference between Silver and Gold?"
    a: "Both cost AED 25. The Gold card lets you ride in the Gold Class cabin on the Metro and Tram, where fares are double the standard rate."
  - q: "Do I need a Personal (Blue) card?"
    a: "Only if you want concession fares (students, seniors 60+, People of Determination) or want your balance protected if the card is lost. It costs AED 70 including AED 20 credit."
