You can buy a nol card at a metro ticket office, at a ticket vending machine in a metro station, or at one of more than 2,000 authorised sales outlets. For a Personal card, apply online at nol.rta.ae.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** the fastest option is a ticket vending machine at a metro station. For a Personal card, apply on nol.rta.ae.

</div>

## Where to buy

| Place | Where it is | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Metro ticket office | Metro stations | Staff can top up and register your card |
| Ticket vending machine | Metro stations | Quick purchase, card types can vary |
| Authorised sales outlets | Around metro stations and beyond | More than 2,000 outlets. Ask for the card you want. |
| Personal card | nol.rta.ae | Apply online. Personalised with name and photo. |

Availability varies by station and outlet. If the card you want isn't on offer, try another place or check [nol card types](/nol-card-types).

## Buy at a ticket vending machine

<ol class="steps">
<li>Find a ticket vending machine in a metro station.</li>
<li>Choose the card you want from the options on the screen.</li>
<li>Pay as the screen asks.</li>
<li>Place the card on the reader to check the balance.</li>
<li>Keep your card and receipt.</li>
</ol>

## Buy at a metro ticket office

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to a metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Ask for the card type you want.</li>
<li>Pay, then take your card.</li>
<li>Ask the staff to register the card if you want balance protection. See the <a href="/register-nol-card">register your nol card guide</a>.</li>
</ol>

## Apply for a Personal card online

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a>.</li>
<li>Choose the Personal card application.</li>
<li>Add your name and photo, as the portal asks.</li>
<li>Pay and follow the steps to receive your card.</li>
</ol>

A Personal card is personalised, and its balance can be recovered if it's lost. Concession fares for students, People of Determination, seniors aged 60 and over and social-affairs beneficiaries require a Personal card.

## Prices

| Card | Price (including credit) | Valid | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silver | AED 25 (AED 19 credit) | 5 years | Standard card |
| Gold | AED 25 (AED 19 credit) | 5 years | Gold Class on Metro and Tram. Gold fares are double standard. |
| Personal (Blue) | AED 70 (AED 20 credit) | 5 years | Personalised. Needed for concession fares. |
| Red ticket | AED 2 | 90 days or 10 trips | Paper. One type of transport at a time. |
| nol Travel card | AED 100 (AED 19 credit) | 5 years | Aimed at tourists and residents |

<div class="tip">

**Check the price before you buy.** These are the figures RTA publishes. Check the current figure on rta.ae.

</div>

## Before you buy: checklist

- Decide between a plastic card and a red ticket. Read [nol card types](/nol-card-types) for the differences.
- Check whether you need a Personal card for a concession fare.
- Note the price and the credit included, so you know what you're paying for.
- Plan your first top-up, and check the [recharge guide](/nol-card-recharge) for channel timing.

## Common mistakes

- Paying for a Personal card when a Silver card covers your needs.
- Buying red tickets for daily travel. Red ticket fares are higher than Silver fares.
- Forgetting that an anonymous card holds up to AED 1,000, and its balance can't be recovered if it's lost.

## Which card to buy

- **Daily commute:** a Silver card, or a Personal card if you want protection or a concession fare.
- **Short visit:** a red ticket for a few trips, or a Silver card for more. See the [guide for tourists](/nol-card-for-tourists).
- **Gold Class travel:** a Gold card, if you use the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram.

Not sure which fits? Use the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

If you qualify for a concession fare, check the card rules before you buy. Concession fares need a Personal card, which is personalised with the holder's name and photo. The [discounts guide](/nol-card-discounts) covers the eligible groups.

## Where to get help

- **Ticket offices** at metro stations can help with top-ups, registration and card questions.
- **RTA call centre:** 800 9090.
- **Lost or damaged card?** Read the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card) before you buy a replacement.

## After you buy

Top up your card in any of the channels in the [recharge guide](/nol-card-recharge). If you bought a Silver or Gold card, consider [registering it](/register-nol-card) so the balance is protected if the card is lost.
