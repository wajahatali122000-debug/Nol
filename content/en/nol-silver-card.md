The nol Silver card is RTA's standard card. It costs AED 25, includes AED 19 credit and is valid for 5 years. It pays standard fares and works on the Metro, Tram, bus, water bus and RTA paid parking.

If you ride every day, the Silver card is the one most people choose. You can [compare all nol cards](/nol-card-types) before you decide.

## Silver card at a glance

| Feature | Silver card |
|---|---|
| Price | AED 25 |
| Credit included | AED 19 |
| Validity | 5 years |
| Max balance (anonymous) | AED 1,000 |
| Max balance (registered) | AED 5,000 |
| Concession fares | Not on this card; a Personal (Blue) card is required |
| Works on | Metro, Tram, bus, water bus and RTA paid parking |

## Silver card fares

Silver cards pay standard fares. Dubai has 7 fare zones, and the fare depends on how many zones you pass through.

| Trip | Silver fare |
|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 |

Use the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares) for the full table and examples, or the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to estimate a month of trips.

## Anonymous or registered Silver card

A new Silver card starts as anonymous. You can register it, and RTA says this gives you two protections: the balance is recoverable if the card is lost, and the balance limit rises to AED 5,000.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Register online on the RTA portal, or go to any metro ticket office.</li>
<li>Wait for approval. You will get an SMS with a PIN. Keep the PIN, because it is used for refunds and for managing your cards.</li>
<li>Tap the card at a metro gate to check in, then check out. This activates the Registered status.</li>
</ol>

For more detail, read [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card).

<div class="note">

**Note:** Anonymous cards cannot be traced to an owner. If you expect to keep a large balance, register the card.

</div>

## How to top up a Silver card

RTA lists these top-up channels for nol cards:

- The nol Pay app
- Ticket offices and ticket vending machines
- More than 2,000 authorised sales outlets around metro stations and beyond
- The RTA website, RTA Dubai app and S'hail app

Top-ups made on the RTA website and in the RTA Dubai and S'hail apps need activation. Wait 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap the card at a metro gate, an RTA parking meter or a solar-powered bus machine. Top-ups at a ticket machine or office go on the card straight away.

<div class="warn">

**Top-up not showing?** This is usually the activation wait. Read [nol balance not updated](/nol-balance-not-updated) for the other checks.

</div>

## Silver vs Gold

Silver and Gold cards cost the same. The difference is the fares and the cabin:

- **Silver:** standard fares, AED 3 / 5 / 7.50.
- **Gold:** double fares, AED 6 / 10 / 15, and access to the Gold Class cabin on Metro and Tram.

If you travel on Metro and do not need the cabin, Silver costs less. If you want the cabin, the Gold card is the one to get. Read the [Gold card guide](/nol-gold-card) for more.

## Who should get a Silver card?

A Silver card suits you if:

- You ride the Metro, Tram or bus most days.
- You want the lowest standard fares.
- You do not qualify for concession fares, or you do not need a Personal card.

If you are visiting for a short stay, a [Red ticket](/nol-red-ticket) may be cheaper. Compare the options on the [card types page](/nol-card-types).

## Quick answers

- **Price:** AED 25, with AED 19 credit.
- **Validity:** 5 years.
- **Max balance:** AED 1,000 anonymous, AED 5,000 registered.
- **Top-ups:** through the nol Pay app, ticket machines, offices and authorised outlets.
