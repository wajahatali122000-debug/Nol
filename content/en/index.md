Want to know how much is left on your nol card before you tap in? You can check it in under a minute — online with the number on the back of your card, in an app, or at any metro station.

This guide covers every method, what to do when a top-up doesn't show, and how far your balance will take you.


## The 5 ways to check your nol balance

| Method | What you need | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| RTA website (nol.rta.ae / rta.ae) | 10-digit tag ID | Fast check from any device |
| nol Pay app | Phone (NFC for card reading) | Regular riders |
| RTA Dubai app | Tag ID | People who already use the app |
| Ticket vending machine | The card | At a metro station |
| Metro gate, bus or parking reader | The card | Seeing balance as you travel |

## 1. Check nol balance online (RTA website)

This is the quickest method and works on any phone or laptop.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the official nol site at <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a> or the nol section of rta.ae.</li>
<li>Choose the balance check option.</li>
<li>Type the <strong>10-digit nol tag ID</strong> from the back of your card.</li>
<li>Submit. Your current balance is shown on screen.</li>
</ol>

Not sure which number to use? See [where to find your nol card number](/find-nol-card-number).

<div class="warn">

**Stay safe:** the balance check only needs your tag ID. Never enter a PIN or bank details on a site that isn't rta.ae or nol.rta.ae.

</div>

## 2. Check balance in the nol Pay app

nol Pay is RTA's dedicated nol app. Besides checking your balance, you can top up, and on supported Android phones you can hold the card against the back of the phone so the app reads it over NFC.

It's the best option if you travel every day. Read our [nol Pay app guide](/nol-pay-app) for set-up steps.

## 3. Check balance in the RTA Dubai app

The RTA Dubai app (also used for parking, Salik and fines) has a nol section. Add your card using the tag ID and the balance shows in the app.

## 4. Check at a ticket vending machine

Every metro station has ticket vending machines. Place your card on the reader and the screen shows your balance. You can top up on the same machine straight away.

## 5. Check at the metro gate, bus or parking meter

When you tap your nol card at a metro gate, on a bus, or at an RTA parking meter, the reader shows your remaining balance. It's not a planned "check", but it's the easiest way to keep an eye on your credit as you travel.

## Top-up not showing in your balance?

This is the most common nol problem, and it's usually normal. RTA says top-ups made on the **RTA website** and in the **RTA Dubai** and **S'hail** apps need activation:

1. Wait **45 minutes to 4 hours** after paying.
2. Tap your card at a **metro gate**, an **RTA parking meter** or a **solar-powered bus machine**.
3. The new amount is written to the card and your balance updates.

Top-ups at a ticket machine or ticket office go on the card immediately. More fixes are in [nol balance not updated](/nol-balance-not-updated).

## How far will your balance go?

Fares depend on how many zones you travel through. These are RTA's standard fares for a Silver card (the Blue Personal card costs the same):

| Trip | Silver / Personal | Gold Class | Red ticket |
|---|---|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 | AED 6 | AED 4 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 | AED 10 | AED 6 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 | AED 15 | AED 8.50 |

So AED 50 covers between about 6 and 16 standard trips. Use our [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to work out a monthly top-up, or see the full [nol fares guide](/nol-card-fares).

## Balance limits by card

- **Anonymous Silver or Gold card:** up to **AED 1,000** balance.
- **Personal (Blue) or registered card:** up to **AED 5,000** balance.

If you keep a large balance, register your card. If a registered or personal card is lost, you can block it and get the balance back — anonymous cards don't have that protection. See [lost nol card](/lost-nol-card).

## Keep your balance topped up

Low on credit? You can [recharge your nol card](/nol-card-recharge) in the nol Pay app, on the RTA website, at ticket machines and offices, or at authorised outlets around the city.
