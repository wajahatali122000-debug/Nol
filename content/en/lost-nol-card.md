If you lose your nol card, submit a refund request as soon as you can. The request blocks the card automatically. If the card was registered or is a personal (Blue) card, you can recover the remaining balance as cash or move it to another card.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** submit a refund request on the RTA website, at a Smart Kiosk or in the nol Pay app. This blocks the lost card. Registered and personal cards keep their balance; anonymous cards can't be recovered.

</div>

<!-- cta -->

## Can you recover the balance?

Only cards that can be traced to an owner can be recovered. Check which type you hold.

| Card type | Can the balance be recovered? |
|---|---|
| Anonymous Silver or Gold | No. Anonymous cards can't be traced to an owner. |
| Registered Silver or Gold | Yes. |
| Personal (Blue) card | Yes. The card is personalised with your name and photo. |

Registering protects the balance if the card is lost. If you haven't registered yet, read [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card). Not sure which card type you have? Use the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card).

## Protect your balance before you lose a card

Keep these limits in mind when you decide how much to load:

| Card type | Maximum balance |
|---|---|
| Anonymous card | AED 1,000 |
| Registered or personal card | AED 5,000 |

If you keep a large balance, register the card first. A card you never register can't be traced, so any balance on it is at risk. See the full [maximum balance guide](/nol-card-maximum-balance) for more detail.

## How to block a lost nol card

Submitting a refund request is how you block the card. RTA creates the block record automatically when the request is submitted.

<ol class="steps">
<li>Go to the nol section of <a href="https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/public-transport/nol/nol-how" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">rta.ae</a>, use a Smart Kiosk, or open the nol Pay app if you hold a virtual nol card.</li>
<li>Choose the refund option and select that the card is lost.</li>
<li>Enter the card details asked for. Have your registration PIN ready if you have one, because the PIN is used for refunds.</li>
<li>Submit the request. The card is blocked once the request goes in.</li>
</ol>

Block the card as soon as you notice it's missing. Don't wait to see whether it turns up.

<div class="warn">

**Stay safe:** never share your PIN or card details on a site that isn't rta.ae or nol.rta.ae. This guide will never ask for your card number.

</div>

## Choose how to get the balance back

You can receive the balance in one of two ways:

- **Cash collection.** Collect the balance as cash.
- **Transfer to another card.** Move the balance to another registered or personal nol card. Check [nol card types](/nol-card-types) to pick one.

### Transfer checklist

<ol class="steps">
<li>Choose transfer when you submit the refund request.</li>
<li>Have a registered or personal nol card ready to receive the balance.</li>
<li>Tap the receiving card at a parking meter, a solar-powered bus machine or a metro gate. The balance is only transferred once the receiving card is tapped.</li>
</ol>

## Get a new card to keep travelling

Your lost card can't be used, so you'll need another one. Compare the options on [nol card types](/nol-card-types). Occasional riders may prefer a [Red ticket](/nol-red-ticket), which is paper and pay-per-trip.

## If your card was anonymous

An anonymous card can't be traced, so RTA can't recover the balance on it. You can still submit a refund request to create the block record for the lost card. Then buy a new card from one of the [places where you can buy a nol card](/where-to-buy-nol-card). Register the new card straight away, so its balance is protected if it's lost too.

## What to have ready

| Item | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Card type (anonymous, registered or personal) | Decides whether the balance can be recovered |
| Registration PIN, if you have one | Used for refunds |
| Cash or receiving card | Needed if you choose a transfer |
| Date you lost the card | Useful if you call RTA |

## Questions about your card?

Call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**. Have your card type and the date you lost the card ready. For the full refund rules, read the [nol card refund guide](/nol-card-refund). If your balance is not showing after a top-up, see [nol balance not updated](/nol-balance-not-updated).
