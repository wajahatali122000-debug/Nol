Reports say RTA plans a new nol–Jaywan card, developed with Al Etihad Payments, with a pilot from November 2026. Khaleej Times and Gulf News have covered the details. RTA has not confirmed the key facts yet. If you hold a nol card today, keep using it.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** nothing has changed for your current card. Keep using it, top up as normal, and check rta.ae for confirmed news.

</div>

## What is reported

The details below come from press reports. They are not confirmed by RTA.

| Item | What the reports say | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Card | A next-generation card from RTA | [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/transport/dubai-launch-nol-jaywan-cards-pilot-phase-november-2026) and [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/dubai/dubais-new-nol-jaywan-card-what-changes-for-users-1.500735515) |
| Partner | Al Etihad Payments, a subsidiary of the Central Bank | Same reports |
| Timing | A pilot planned from November 2026 | Same reports |
| First phase | Public transport, taxis and public parking | Same reports |
| Later phases | More services added over time | Same reports |
| Discounts | More than 120 discounts | Same reports |
| Activation | Through a smartphone, on Android and iOS | Same reports |

Jaywan is the UAE's national domestic card scheme. The nol–Jaywan card is reported to be part of that scheme.

## What is not confirmed

Treat these points as open until RTA announces them:

- The exact launch date
- The price of the card
- Whether existing nol cards must be replaced
- How taxi payments work with current nol cards
- The timing of later phases

<div class="warn">

**Be careful:** some sites may claim early sign-up or ask for payment before any launch. Check rta.ae first. This guide will never ask for your card number.

</div>

## How to tell confirmed news from reports

- **Confirmed:** RTA announces it on rta.ae or through its own channels.
- **Reported:** a news outlet publishes it, citing sources. It may change.
- **Unconfirmed:** any launch date, price or sign-up page that doesn't appear on rta.ae.

## If you hear about a new card in your area

Follow the same steps as above. If you haven't registered your current card yet, do it now. See [how to register your nol card](/register-nol-card). Registration protects the balance whichever card you use in the future.

## Questions to ask RTA

For confirmed answers, call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**, or check rta.ae. Ask about:

- The launch date in your area
- Whether your current card needs to be replaced
- The card price and how to get one
