This policy explains how Nol Balance Guide handles information when you visit the site. We keep it simple: the site is a static guide, and we don't ask you to create an account.

## What we don't collect

- We don't have user accounts or logins.
- We don't collect nol card numbers, tag IDs, PINs or bank details. Please never send them to us.

## Anonymous analytics

We may use anonymous analytics to see which pages are read and how the site performs. This data doesn't identify you personally.

## Google AdSense and cookies

This site may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites.

- You can manage personalised ads in Google's ad settings at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
- To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, read [Google's ad policy page](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

## Links to other sites

This site links to rta.ae, nol.rta.ae and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We aren't responsible for how they handle your information.

## Your choices

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings.
- Avoid typing personal details into any form. This site doesn't ask for them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest update.
