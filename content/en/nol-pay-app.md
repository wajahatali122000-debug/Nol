nol Pay is RTA's app for the nol card. Use it to top up your card and to manage a virtual nol card on your phone. It's a good choice if you travel often and want to avoid a trip to a ticket office.

<div class="note">

**Get the app:** open the [nol Pay page on rta.ae](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/smart-apps/app-details/nol+pay/nol-pay) and follow the link from there.

</div>

## What nol Pay does

| Feature | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Top-up | Add credit to your nol card from your phone |
| Virtual nol card | Use nol on your phone, without a plastic card |
| Refunds | Report a lost, damaged or faulty virtual card and apply for a refund |

## Set up nol Pay

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the nol Pay page on rta.ae and follow the link to the app.</li>
<li>Install the app on your phone.</li>
<li>Follow the prompts to add your nol card or virtual nol card.</li>
<li>Top up when your balance is low, then check that the new amount shows.</li>
</ol>

## nol Pay vs other top-up channels

| Channel | Activation wait for top-ups | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| nol Pay app | Not stated on RTA's nol pages | Managing your card on your phone |
| RTA website | 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap | Topping up from a laptop |
| RTA Dubai app | 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap | People who already use the app |
| S'hail app | 45 minutes to 4 hours, then tap | People who already use the app |
| Ticket machine or office | None, instant | Topping up on the spot |

For the full list, read the [nol card recharge guide](/nol-card-recharge).

## About the virtual nol card

A virtual nol card lives in the app instead of on plastic. You still use nol for your trips, and the balance sits with the card in nol Pay.

If something goes wrong with a virtual card, report it in the app and apply for a refund. The refund rules are the same as for plastic cards. Read the [nol card refund guide](/nol-card-refund) for the options, including cash collection or a balance transfer to another registered card.

## Check the app's balance

If your top-up doesn't show after payment, the timing depends on the channel you used. RTA's nol pages give a 45-minute to 4-hour wait for online top-ups from the RTA website and the RTA Dubai and S'hail apps. For nol Pay, RTA's pages don't give a separate figure. If the balance is still wrong, follow the [balance not updated guide](/nol-balance-not-updated) or call RTA on 800 9090.

## Who nol Pay suits

- **Regular riders** who want to top up without visiting a ticket office.
- **Virtual card users** who don't want to carry a plastic card.
- **Anyone managing nol tasks online.** The nol portal at nol.rta.ae is where you apply, personalise, top up and renew a travel pass.

## Getting started checklist

- Download the app from the [nol Pay page on rta.ae](https://www.rta.ae/wps/portal/rta/ae/home/smart-apps/app-details/nol+pay/nol-pay).
- Add your card, or set up your virtual nol card, following the prompts.
- Check the balance once to confirm the card is showing.
- Top up only the amount you need for the next few trips.
- Register the card if you plan to keep it, so the balance is protected.

## What to check in the app

- Your balance after each top-up, before you travel.
- The refund steps for a lost, damaged or faulty virtual card.
- The timing of any top-up you made through another channel, if it's missing.

## Protect your balance

A registered card can be traced to its owner, so the balance is protected if the card is lost. Register your card, then check the limit on the [maximum balance page](/nol-card-maximum-balance). Full steps are in the [register your nol card guide](/register-nol-card).

## Check your fares before you top up

Work out how much credit you need with the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator). Standard Silver fares are AED 3, AED 5 or AED 7.50 per trip, depending on how many zones you cross.

## If the app doesn't show what you expect

- **Balance looks old.** Compare the figure with the last top-up, then check the timing rules in the [balance not updated guide](/nol-balance-not-updated).
- **Card missing from the app.** Check that you entered the 10-digit tag ID correctly. See [where to find your nol card number](/find-nol-card-number).
- **Card lost or faulty.** Report it and apply for a refund. The [nol card refund guide](/nol-card-refund) explains the options.
