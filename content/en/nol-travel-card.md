The nol Travel card costs AED 100, includes AED 19 credit and is valid for 5 years. RTA aims it at tourists and residents and runs a separate site for it, noltravel.ae. For the full terms, check that site, because this guide does not list extra benefits.

Before you buy, compare it with the cheaper [Silver card](/nol-silver-card) and the [Red ticket](/nol-red-ticket). All cards are listed on the [card types page](/nol-card-types).

## Travel card vs Silver card

Both cards include AED 19 credit and last 5 years. The main difference is the price and how RTA markets them.

| Feature | nol Travel card | Silver card |
|---|---|---|
| Price | AED 100 | AED 25 |
| Credit included | AED 19 | AED 19 |
| Validity | 5 years | 5 years |
| RTA details | noltravel.ae | rta.ae |

Because the price is much higher, read the terms on noltravel.ae before buying. If you only need a card for a short trip, the Red ticket may cost less.

## Where to check the full details

RTA publishes the details for the Travel card on its own site. When you visit noltravel.ae, look for:

- The current price and what it includes.
- How to top up the card and where.
- How to check your balance.
- What happens if the card is lost or damaged.
- Any other benefits RTA lists for the card.

We have not listed these in this guide, because we could not confirm them from RTA's published pages. If the site and this guide disagree, follow the site.

## Fares when you use a nol card

Fares depend on how many zones you pass through. Dubai has 7 fare zones. The standard fares on Silver and Personal cards are:

- Within 1 zone: AED 3
- 2 adjacent zones: AED 5
- More than 2 zones: AED 7.50

These are the standard figures for Silver and Personal cards. Check [nol fares on rta.ae](/nol-card-fares) for the full table, or use the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to estimate your trips.

## Is the Travel card right for you?

The Travel card may suit you if:

- You are visiting and want an RTA card with a 5-year validity.
- You want the details and terms on RTA's dedicated Travel card site.

Another card may suit you better if:

- You want the cheapest way to start. The Silver card costs AED 25.
- You only need a few trips. A Red ticket costs AED 2 and works for 10 trips or 90 days.
- You need a personalised card or concession fares. A Personal (Blue) card is required for concessions.

## Keeping your card topped up

Whatever card you choose, keep your balance above the fare for your next trip. RTA does not list a minimum balance on its nol pages, so check your balance before you travel. Read [how to recharge a nol card](/nol-card-recharge) for the top-up options, and [nol card for tourists](/nol-card-for-tourists) for more visitor information.
