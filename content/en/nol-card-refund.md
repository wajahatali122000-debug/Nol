A nol card refund returns the balance on a card you no longer use or can't use. You can request one if your card is lost, damaged, faulty or expired. You can take the balance as cash, or transfer it to another registered or personal nol card.

<div class="note">

**Quick answer:** request the refund on the RTA website, at a Smart Kiosk or in the nol Pay app. Choose cash or transfer. Anonymous cards that are lost can't be recovered.

</div>

## Reasons you can request a refund

| Reason | What it covers | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Lost | The card is missing or stolen | Only registered and personal cards can be recovered. See [lost nol card](/lost-nol-card). |
| Damaged | The card is physically harmed | Apply through the same channels as a lost card. |
| Faulty | The card doesn't work properly | Apply through the same channels. |
| Expired | The card is past its validity | Cards are valid for a set period. See [expiry and renewal](/nol-card-expiry-renewal). |

## Where to apply

You can submit a refund request in three places:

- **RTA website.** Use the nol section of rta.ae.
- **Smart Kiosks.** Use the kiosk to start the request.
- **nol Pay app.** This is for virtual nol card holders only. Read the [nol Pay app guide](/nol-pay-app) to set it up.

## Step by step

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the RTA website, a Smart Kiosk or the nol Pay app.</li>
<li>Choose the refund option and select the reason: lost, damaged, faulty or expired.</li>
<li>Enter the card details asked for. Have your registration PIN ready if you have one, because the PIN is used for refunds and managing cards.</li>
<li>Choose how you want the balance back: cash collection or transfer to another card.</li>
<li>Submit the request. A block record is created on the card automatically.</li>
</ol>

<div class="warn">

**Stay safe:** only enter your details on rta.ae, nol.rta.ae or the nol Pay app. Never share your PIN with anyone else.

</div>

## Cash or transfer?

| Option | What happens | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Cash collection | You collect the balance in cash | People who don't plan to use another nol card |
| Transfer | The balance moves to another registered or personal card | People who want to keep the value on nol |

### How a transfer works

A transfer only works with a **registered or personal** nol card. Anonymous cards can't receive a transfer under this process. If you don't have one, register a card first using the steps in [register your nol card](/register-nol-card).

### The tap rule

When you choose a transfer, the receiving card must be tapped to receive the balance. Use any one of these:

- A RTA parking meter
- A solar-powered bus machine
- A metro gate

Until the receiving card is tapped, the transfer is not complete on that card.

## Registered and anonymous cards

Registration makes a real difference when you ask for a refund:

- **Registered or personal card:** the card can be traced to its owner. The balance can be recovered if the card is lost.
- **Anonymous card:** the card can't be traced. If it's lost, the balance can't be recovered.

If you hold a large balance, check the [maximum balance rules](/nol-card-maximum-balance) before you load more.

## Fees and processing times

RTA shows the current fees and timelines during the refund request. This guide doesn't list them, because the figures can change. Check the current figures on rta.ae before you submit. If you have questions, call the RTA call centre on **800 9090**.

## Compare your options

Not sure which card to get after a refund? Use the [which nol card tool](/tools/which-nol-card) to compare card types. You can also check the [nol fares guide](/nol-card-fares) to see what a trip costs on each card.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a transfer but forgetting to tap the receiving card.
- Expecting a transfer from an anonymous card, which can't be traced to an owner.
- Assuming the money arrives at once. Check the timing on rta.ae.

## Questions to ask before you apply

- Is my card registered or personal?
- Do I want cash, or do I want to move the balance to another card?
- Do I have a receiving card ready, and can I tap it at a meter, bus machine or metro gate?

If your refund is for a lost card, read the full [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card) first.
