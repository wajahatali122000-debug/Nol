The nol Personal (Blue) card costs AED 70, includes AED 20 credit and is valid for 5 years. It is personalised with your name and photo, its balance can be recovered if the card is lost, and it is required for concession fares.

You can apply on the nol portal at [nol.rta.ae](https://nol.rta.ae/) or at a metro ticket office. For the other card options, see the [card types comparison](/nol-card-types).

## Personal (Blue) card at a glance

| Feature | Personal (Blue) card |
|---|---|
| Price | AED 70 |
| Credit included | AED 20 |
| Validity | 5 years |
| Personalised | Yes, with name and photo |
| Balance recoverable if lost | Yes |
| Concession fares | Yes, required for them |
| Max balance | AED 5,000 |

## How to apply

Personal cards are applied for through RTA's nol channels. Here is the general route:

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the nol portal at <a href="https://nol.rta.ae/" target="_blank" rel="noopener nofollow">nol.rta.ae</a> and find the option to apply for a personalised card.</li>
<li>Or go to any metro ticket office and ask for a Personal (Blue) card.</li>
<li>Check the current documents you need on rta.ae before you go. We do not list them here because RTA's requirements can change.</li>
<li>Once your card is issued, top it up through the nol Pay app, ticket machines or authorised outlets.</li>
</ol>

<div class="tip">

**Not sure about the documents?** Call the RTA call centre on 800 9090 and ask what you need to bring for a Personal card.

</div>

## Why the Personal card matters for concessions

RTA requires a Personal card for concession fares. These concession groups are listed on RTA's site:

- Students
- People of Determination
- Seniors aged 60 and over
- Social-affairs beneficiaries

RTA does not publish the discount percentage on these pages, so we do not state one. Read [nol card discounts](/nol-card-discounts) for how to check eligibility.

## Personal card vs Silver card

A Personal card and a Silver card both pay standard fares, but they differ in several ways:

- **Price:** Personal costs AED 70 with AED 20 credit. Silver costs AED 25 with AED 19 credit.
- **Concessions:** Personal is required. Silver does not give you concession fares.
- **Balance limit:** Both can hold up to AED 5,000 once registered or personal.
- **Protection:** Personal balances can be recovered if the card is lost.

If you do not need the personalised card or a concession, the [Silver card](/nol-silver-card) is cheaper to buy.

## Protecting your Personal card

RTA says that a lost, damaged, faulty or expired nol card can be reported and refunded through the RTA website, Smart Kiosks or the nol Pay app (for virtual nol card holders). Read the [lost nol card guide](/lost-nol-card) for the steps. A personal card's balance can be traced to you, so keep your details up to date.

## Fares on a Personal card

Personal cards pay the same standard fares as Silver cards:

| Trip | Personal fare |
|---|---|
| Within 1 zone | AED 3 |
| 2 adjacent zones | AED 5 |
| More than 2 zones | AED 7.50 |

Use the [nol card fares guide](/nol-card-fares) for all card fares, or the [fare calculator](/tools/fare-calculator) to estimate your monthly cost.
